中文面試

請先自我介紹。
我畢業於數位媒體設計系，職涯從影音與設計開始，後來轉向數位廣告投放，累積約兩年半的成效分析與優化經驗，目前在 MyCard 接觸更完整的行銷企劃。這些經驗讓我希望補強消費者行為、研究方法與管理決策。
為什麼工作後還要讀研究所？
工作讓我累積實務判斷，但有時知道怎麼做有效，卻還不夠有系統地回答為什麼有效。我希望把經驗轉成更有架構、能驗證的決策能力。
你的研究題目一句話怎麼說？
我想了解不同參與程度的會員，是否需要搭配不同的關係溝通方式，才能提高持續參與意願。

English Interview

Tell me about yourself.
I graduated with a degree in Digital Media Design. I started my career in video production and later moved into digital advertising. I gained about two and a half years of experience in campaign optimization and data analysis. I now work in marketing at MyCard.
Why do you want to pursue a master's degree?
My work experience taught me how to solve practical problems, but I also want to understand why certain strategies work. I hope graduate school can help me build stronger research and strategic thinking skills.
What is your research topic?
My research focuses on how member participation patterns and relationship strategies may influence members' intention to stay engaged.
