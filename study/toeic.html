原創題型練習

Part 2｜When will the new schedule be announced?
A. By Friday. B. In the main lobby. C. Mr. Chen did.
答案 A：When 問時間。
Part 5｜All applicants are required ___ the form by noon.
A submit / B to submit / C submitted / D submitting
答案 B：be required to + V。
Part 5｜The manager will ___ the final budget tomorrow.
A approval / B approve / C approved / D approving
答案 B：will + 原形動詞。
Part 5｜The shipment was delayed ___ bad weather.
A because / B because of / C although / D during
答案 B：後面是名詞片語 bad weather。
Part 7｜Notice: The office will close at 3 P.M. Friday for maintenance. When will it close?
答案：3 P.M. on Friday。先抓題目問的時間，再回原文定位。
